Question:
Why are B+ Trees substantially superior to standard B-Trees for database range queries (e.g., SELECT WHERE key BETWEEN x AND y)?

Options:
A. Leaf nodes are linked in a doubly or singly linked list, allowing sequential range traversal without tree re-traversal
B. B+ Trees do not require sorting
C. Internal nodes in B+ Trees are kept on disk while leaves stay in cache
D. B+ Trees allow direct hash lookups on leaves

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Once the start key is located via root-to-leaf search, the range query simply iterates through the linked list of leaf pages without traversing back up internal nodes.
