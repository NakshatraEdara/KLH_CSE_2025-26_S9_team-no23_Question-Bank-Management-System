Question:
What algorithm finds an Eulerian circuit in a graph in O(V + E) time?

Options:
A. Hierholzer's algorithm
B. Bellman-Ford algorithm
C. Kruskal's algorithm
D. Hopcroft-Karp algorithm

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Hierholzer's algorithm finds an Eulerian path/circuit in O(E) time by tracing sub-tours until edges are exhausted, splicing sub-tours into the main tour.
