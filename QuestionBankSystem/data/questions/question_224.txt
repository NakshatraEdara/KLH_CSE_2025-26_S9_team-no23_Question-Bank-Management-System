Question:
What is the Difference Constraint System, and how can it be mapped to a graph algorithm instead of pure exponential backtracking?

Options:
A. Constraints x_j - x_i <= w_k can be mapped directly to directed edges from i to j with weight w_k and solved via Bellman-Ford in O(V * E) time
B. It is mapped to Huffman coding
C. It is mapped to QuickSort
D. It is mapped to binary indexed trees

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Systems of difference constraints match the triangle inequality of shortest paths: dist[j] <= dist[i] + w(i, j). Running Bellman-Ford detects feasibility or negative cycles in O(V * E) polynomial time.
