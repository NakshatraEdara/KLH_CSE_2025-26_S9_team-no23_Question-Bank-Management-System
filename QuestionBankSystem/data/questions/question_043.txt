Question:
When deleting a node with two children from a BST, which node can safely replace it without violating the BST property?

Options:
A. Its in-order predecessor or in-order successor
B. The root of the tree
C. The leftmost leaf of the entire tree
D. Any random leaf node

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The in-order successor (minimum in right subtree) or in-order predecessor (maximum in left subtree) has at most one child and preserves all ordering invariants when swapped into the target node.
