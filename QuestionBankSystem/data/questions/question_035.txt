Question:
What is the purpose of using 3-way partitioning (Dutch National Flag partitioning) in QuickSort?

Options:
A. To handle inputs with many duplicate keys in O(n) time
B. To sort three different arrays simultaneously
C. To eliminate the need for recursion completely
D. To avoid comparing elements with the pivot

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
3-way partitioning splits the array into < pivot, == pivot, and > pivot. When many duplicate keys exist, elements equal to pivot are excluded from further recursion, achieving O(n) time.
