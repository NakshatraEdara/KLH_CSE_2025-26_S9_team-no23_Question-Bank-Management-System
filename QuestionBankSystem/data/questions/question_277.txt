Question:
In the traceback step of the Needleman-Wunsch algorithm, which matrix move corresponds to an insertion in the first sequence (or deletion in the second)?

Options:
A. A vertical move (from cell (i, j) to (i - 1, j))
B. A diagonal move (from (i, j) to (i - 1, j - 1))
C. A horizontal move (from (i, j) to (i, j - 1))
D. A jump to the root

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A vertical move decrements i while leaving j unchanged, aligning character s1[i] with a gap '-'. A horizontal move aligns a gap '-' with s2[j].
