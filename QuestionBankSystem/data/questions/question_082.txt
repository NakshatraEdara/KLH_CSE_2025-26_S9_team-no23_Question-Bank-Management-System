Question:
In a 1-indexed Fenwick tree, which bitwise expression isolates the lowest set bit (least significant 1-bit) of an integer index i?

Options:
A. i & (-i)
B. i | (-i)
C. i ^ (-i)
D. ~i

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
In two's complement representation, -i equals (~i) + 1. Performing bitwise AND between i and -i zeroes all bits except the lowest set bit.
