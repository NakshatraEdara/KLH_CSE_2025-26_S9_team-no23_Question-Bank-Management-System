Question:
What is the update loop step for index i in a 1-indexed Fenwick tree of size n?

Options:
A. i += (i & -i) while i <= n
B. i -= (i & -i) while i > 0
C. i *= 2 while i <= n
D. i = i / 2 while i > 0

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
In point updates, adding (i & -i) propagates changes to all responsible ancestor intervals up to the array bound n.
