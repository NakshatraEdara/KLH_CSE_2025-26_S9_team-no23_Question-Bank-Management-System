Question:
What does the Cycle Property of Minimum Spanning Trees state?

Options:
A. For any cycle in the graph, the strictly heaviest edge in the cycle cannot belong to any MST
B. Every cycle must have at least one MST edge
C. All cycles have equal total weight
D. The MST must contain a cycle if V > 10

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
If an edge e is the strictly heaviest edge in a cycle C, removing e and replacing it with any other edge of C reconnects the components with smaller total weight, proving e cannot be in any MST.
