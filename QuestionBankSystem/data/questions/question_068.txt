Question:
In Open Addressing, how does Quadratic Probing calculate the probe sequence for probe number i (0 <= i < m)?

Options:
A. (hash(key) + c1 * i + c2 * i^2) % m
B. (hash(key) + i) % m
C. (hash(key) * i^2) % m
D. (hash(key) + 2^i) % m

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Quadratic probing computes probe index as (h(k) + c1*i + c2*i^2) % m. This breaks up primary clusters, although identical initial hashes follow identical paths (secondary clustering).
