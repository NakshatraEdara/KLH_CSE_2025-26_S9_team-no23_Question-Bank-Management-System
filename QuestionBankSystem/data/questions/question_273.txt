Question:
What is the Damerau-Levenshtein distance, and how does it extend standard Levenshtein distance?

Options:
A. It adds transposition of two adjacent characters as a fourth primitive edit operation costing 1
B. It allows deleting entire words at once
C. It excludes substitutions
D. It only counts insertions

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Damerau-Levenshtein distance accounts for common human typographical errors by treating the adjacent transposition of two characters (e.g. 'teh' -> 'the') as a single edit step.
