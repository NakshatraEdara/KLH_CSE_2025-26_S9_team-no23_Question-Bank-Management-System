Question:
What is the average-case and worst-case time complexity of the Rabin-Karp algorithm for text length n and pattern length m?

Options:
A. Average: O(n + m), Worst-case: O(n * m)
B. Average: O(n * m), Worst-case: O(n + m)
C. Both are O(n log m)
D. Both are O(n^2)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
With a good hash function, hash collisions are rare, yielding O(n + m) average time. If pathological collisions occur for every window, full character verification takes O(n * m) worst case.
