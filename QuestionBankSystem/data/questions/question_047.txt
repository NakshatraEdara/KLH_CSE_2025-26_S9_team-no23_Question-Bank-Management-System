Question:
What is the maximum height of an AVL tree containing n nodes in terms of big-O notation?

Options:
A. O(log n)
B. O(sqrt(n))
C. O(n)
D. O(n log n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The minimum number of nodes in an AVL tree of height h follows Fibonacci-like growth N(h) = N(h-1) + N(h-2) + 1 ~ phi^h, meaning h is bounded by ~1.44 log2(n), which is O(log n).
