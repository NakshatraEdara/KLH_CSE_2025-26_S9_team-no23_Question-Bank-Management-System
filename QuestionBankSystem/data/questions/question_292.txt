Question:
How does Circulation with Demands and Edge Lower Bounds differ from standard maximum flow?

Options:
A. Each edge has both a lower bound l(e) and upper bound c(e) (l(e) <= f(e) <= c(e)), and intermediate vertices have net supplies or demands
B. Edges can have negative capacities
C. Vertices can destroy flow
D. Flow cannot be represented by integers

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Circulation problems enforce minimum throughput constraints l(u, v) on edges. Transformed networks route initial lower bound flows and use auxiliary source/sink vertices to satisfy residual balances.
