Question:
What sentinel character convention is commonly appended to a string when constructing its suffix array?

Options:
A. A unique termination character (e.g. '$') lexicographically smaller than any character in the alphabet
B. A space character
C. A null character that is deleted immediately
D. The character 'z'

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Appending a unique sentinel '$' that is smaller than all alphabet symbols ensures that no suffix is a proper prefix of any other suffix, simplifying suffix sorting.
