Question:
What is the theoretical time complexity of Prim's algorithm when using a Fibonacci Heap?

Options:
A. O(E + V log V)
B. O(E log V)
C. O(V^2)
D. O(E * V)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Fibonacci heaps support decrease-key in O(1) amortized time and extract-min in O(log V) amortized time, leading to O(V log V + E) overall time, which is optimal for dense graphs.
