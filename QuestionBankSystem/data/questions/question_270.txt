Question:
What is Hirschberg's algorithm, and what remarkable efficiency does it achieve for sequence alignment?

Options:
A. A divide-and-conquer algorithm that reconstructs the optimal alignment in O(m * n) time while using only O(min(m, n)) linear space
B. An algorithm that aligns sequences in O(1) space and O(n) time
C. A randomized approximation algorithm
D. A hardware-only alignment chip

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Hirschberg's algorithm splits the problem at row m/2, computes forward and backward score vectors in linear space, identifies the midpoint column intersection, and recurses, achieving full alignment in O(min(m, n)) space.
