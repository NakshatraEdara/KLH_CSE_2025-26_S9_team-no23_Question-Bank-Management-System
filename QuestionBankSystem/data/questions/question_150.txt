Question:
If an existing MST has been computed for a graph, and the weight of one non-MST edge e=(u, v) is decreased, how fast can the new MST be updated?

Options:
A. O(V) time (or O(log V) with dynamic tree structures) by checking the maximum edge on the path between u and v in the MST
B. O(E log V) by recomputing from scratch
C. O(V^3)
D. O(1) unconditionally

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Adding edge e creates a unique fundamental cycle in the MST. Finding the max edge on the tree path between u and v in O(V) and replacing it if it is heavier than e's new weight updates the MST.
