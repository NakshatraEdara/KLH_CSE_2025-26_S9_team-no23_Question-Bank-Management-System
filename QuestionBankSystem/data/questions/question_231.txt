Question:
What is the time complexity to compute the complete Z-array of a string of length n using the Z-algorithm?

Options:
A. O(n) linear time
B. O(n^2)
C. O(n log n)
D. O(log n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The Z-algorithm maintains a 'Z-box' interval [L, R] of the rightmost matched segment. By reusing previously computed Z-values inside [L, R], the right pointer R advances monotonically, achieving O(n) time.
