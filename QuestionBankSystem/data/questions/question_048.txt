Question:
Given the pre-order traversal sequence [50, 30, 20, 40, 70, 60, 80], how can the unique BST be reconstructed in O(n) time?

Options:
A. By recursively consuming elements with upper and lower value bounds
B. By sorting the array and running Floyd-Warshall
C. By inserting elements backwards into a deque
D. Reconstruction from pre-order alone is mathematically impossible

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Because a BST's in-order traversal is its sorted keys, pre-order elements can be parsed with valid [min, max] range constraints in a single pass of O(n) time.
