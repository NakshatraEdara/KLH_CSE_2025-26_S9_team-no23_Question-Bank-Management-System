Question:
If a B-Tree node has k children, exactly how many keys does it contain?

Options:
A. k - 1 keys
B. k keys
C. k + 1 keys
D. 2 * k keys

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The keys act as dividing boundaries between child pointers. Thus, a node with k children contains exactly k - 1 keys.
