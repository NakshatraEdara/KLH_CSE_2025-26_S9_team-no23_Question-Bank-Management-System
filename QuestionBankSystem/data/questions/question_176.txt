Question:
What is Warshall's Transitive Closure algorithm, and how does it relate to Floyd-Warshall?

Options:
A. It computes reachability (reach[i][j] = reach[i][j] || (reach[i][k] && reach[k][j])) using bitwise boolean operations
B. It computes minimum cuts
C. It finds topological sorts
D. It computes maximum flow

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Warshall's algorithm is the boolean precursor to Floyd-Warshall: replacing min with OR and addition with AND computes whether a directed path exists between any two vertices.
