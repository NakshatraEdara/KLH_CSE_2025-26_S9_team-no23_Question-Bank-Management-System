Question:
In the Monotonic Stack algorithm for finding the Next Greater Element for all elements in an array of size n, what is the total overall time complexity?

Options:
A. O(n^2)
B. O(n log n)
C. O(n)
D. O(2^n)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation:
Even though a nested while loop pops from the stack, each element is pushed and popped at most once across the entire iteration, resulting in O(n) total amortized time.
