Question:
What is the time and space complexity of computing the n-th Fibonacci number using simple bottom-up DP with two variables?

Options:
A. Time O(n) and Space O(1)
B. Time O(2^n) and Space O(n)
C. Time O(n) and Space O(n)
D. Time O(1) and Space O(n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Because F(n) depends only on F(n-1) and F(n-2), tracking only the previous two values in a loop from 2 to n achieves linear time and constant auxiliary memory.
