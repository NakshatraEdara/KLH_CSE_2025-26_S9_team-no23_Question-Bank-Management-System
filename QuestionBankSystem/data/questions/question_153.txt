Question:
What type of problem does Dijkstra's algorithm solve?

Options:
A. Single-Source Shortest Path (SSSP) on graphs with non-negative edge weights
B. All-Pairs Shortest Paths on graphs with negative cycles
C. Maximum Flow in bipartite networks
D. Topological Sorting of undirected graphs

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Dijkstra's algorithm computes the shortest paths from a single source vertex to all other vertices in a directed or undirected graph with strictly non-negative edge weights.
