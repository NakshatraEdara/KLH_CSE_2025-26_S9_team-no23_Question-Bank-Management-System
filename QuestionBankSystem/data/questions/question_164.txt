Question:
What is the Shortest Path Faster Algorithm (SPFA), and what is its relationship to Bellman-Ford?

Options:
A. A queue-optimized version of Bellman-Ford that only relaxes edges from vertices whose distances were updated in the previous step
B. A replacement for binary search
C. An algorithm that only runs on complete graphs
D. A variant of Prim's algorithm

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
SPFA maintains a FIFO queue of candidate vertices whose distances were recently reduced. It runs in O(E) average time on random graphs, but retains Bellman-Ford's O(V * E) worst-case time.
