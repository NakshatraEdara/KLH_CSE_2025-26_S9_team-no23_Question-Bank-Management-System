Question:
What is the Boyer-Moore string matching algorithm's best-case time complexity for a text of length n and pattern of length m?

Options:
A. O(n / m) sub-linear time
B. O(n + m)
C. O(n * m)
D. O(log n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Boyer-Moore matches characters from right to left in the pattern. Using the bad character rule, mismatches with characters not in the pattern allow skipping m characters at a time, achieving O(n / m) best-case time.
