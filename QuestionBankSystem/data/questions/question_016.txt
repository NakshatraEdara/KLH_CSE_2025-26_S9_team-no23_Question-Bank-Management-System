Question:
What is the time complexity to find the middle element of a singly linked list in a single pass?

Options:
A. O(1)
B. O(n)
C. O(n log n)
D. O(n^2)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
Using a two-pointer technique (slow pointer moving 1 step and fast pointer moving 2 steps), the middle is reached when fast reaches the end in a single pass of O(n) time.
