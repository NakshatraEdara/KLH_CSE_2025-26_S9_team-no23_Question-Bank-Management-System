Question:
What is the Knight's Tour problem, and what heuristic drastically speeds up backtracking search for a valid tour?

Options:
A. Warnsdorff's heuristic: always move the knight to the unvisited adjacent square with the fewest onward legal moves
B. A* search with Manhattan distance
C. Dijkstra's heuristic
D. Always moving to the center of the board

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A Knight's Tour visits every chessboard square exactly once. Warnsdorff's rule greedily picks the square with the minimum outgoing degree, preventing premature isolation of corner squares.
