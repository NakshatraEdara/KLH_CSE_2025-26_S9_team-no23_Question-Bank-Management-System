Question:
What is the Burrows-Wheeler Transform (BWT), and how can it be constructed directly from a Suffix Array SA of text T of length n?

Options:
A. BWT[i] = T[(SA[i] - 1 + n) % n], taking the character immediately preceding each sorted suffix
B. BWT[i] = T[SA[i]]
C. BWT[i] = reverse(T)[SA[i]]
D. BWT is constructed only via Huffman coding

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The BWT is the last column of the sorted matrix of cyclic rotations of T. This corresponds exactly to the character preceding each suffix in the suffix array: T[(SA[i] - 1 + n) % n].
