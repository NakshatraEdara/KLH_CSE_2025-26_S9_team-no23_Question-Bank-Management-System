Question:
In the Subset Sum formulation of building a balanced exam totaling exactly 100 marks from a bank of questions with varied marks, what is the time complexity using 1D dynamic programming?

Options:
A. O(N * M) where N is number of candidate questions and M is target marks (100)
B. O(2^N) strictly
C. O(N^3)
D. O(1)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A boolean DP array dp[m] of size M + 1 (101) updated in reverse for each question takes O(N * M) operations, which executes in microseconds for N=300 and M=100.
