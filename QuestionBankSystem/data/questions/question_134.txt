Question:
In a connected undirected graph with V vertices, what is the minimum number of edges required to keep it connected?

Options:
A. V - 1 edges (forming a spanning tree)
B. V edges
C. V / 2 edges
D. V * (V - 1) / 2 edges

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Any minimally connected undirected graph with V vertices is a tree, which contains exactly V - 1 edges.
