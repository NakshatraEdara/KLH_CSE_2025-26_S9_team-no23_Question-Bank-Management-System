Question:
What is the time complexity of Dinic's algorithm when applied to unit networks (where all capacities are 1, such as standard bipartite matching reductions)?

Options:
A. O(E * sqrt(V))
B. O(V^2 * E)
C. O(V * E)
D. O(V^3)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
On unit networks, the number of Dinic phases is bounded by O(sqrt(V)), and each phase takes O(E) time, resulting in O(E * sqrt(V)) time, which directly matches Hopcroft-Karp matching.
