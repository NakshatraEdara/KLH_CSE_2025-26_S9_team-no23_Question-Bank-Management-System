Question:
What is the time complexity of Breadth-First Search (BFS) on a graph with V vertices and E edges represented using adjacency lists?

Options:
A. O(V + E)
B. O(V * E)
C. O(V^2)
D. O(E log V)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
BFS visits every vertex at most once and inspects every incident edge across all adjacency lists once (or twice for undirected graphs), resulting in O(V + E) time.
