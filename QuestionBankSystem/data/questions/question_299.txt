Question:
When generating multiple balanced exam papers from a question bank, what does the Min-Cost Multi-Commodity Flow or Ford-Fulkerson variant optimize?

Options:
A. Maximal flow verifies if all exam sets can be filled with distinct questions; edge costs can minimize difficulty variance across sets
B. It replaces question text with random characters
C. It sorts students by registration number
D. It guarantees questions have only 2 options

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Modeling question allocation as network flow ensures each question is used at most once (unit edge capacities) while satisfying per-exam quotas; costs penalize deviation from target difficulty profiles.
