Question:
During Z-algorithm execution, if current index i falls inside the current Z-box [L, R], and k = i - L, what are the two main cases?

Options:
A. If Z[k] < R - i + 1, then Z[i] = Z[k]; otherwise Z[i] >= R - i + 1 and must be extended by manual character comparisons from R + 1
B. Z[i] is always equal to Z[k] regardless of R
C. Z[i] is always reset to 0
D. The algorithm resets L and R to 0

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
If the prefix match at k fits strictly inside the remaining interval (Z[k] < R - i + 1), it cannot match beyond R, so Z[i] = Z[k]. If it touches or exceeds R, further characters past R must be checked.
