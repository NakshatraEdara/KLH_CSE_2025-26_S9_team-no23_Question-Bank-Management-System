Question:
What is the balance factor of a node in an AVL Tree?

Options:
A. Height(Left Subtree) - Height(Right Subtree)
B. Number of left descendants - Number of right descendants
C. Key of left child - Key of right child
D. Depth(Left Subtree) + Depth(Right Subtree)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
In an AVL tree, the balance factor of any node is defined as height(left) - height(right) (or vice versa), and must remain in {-1, 0, +1} for every node.
