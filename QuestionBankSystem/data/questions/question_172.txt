Question:
What is the time complexity and space complexity of the Floyd-Warshall algorithm on a graph with V vertices?

Options:
A. Time: O(V^3), Space: O(V^2)
B. Time: O(V^2), Space: O(V)
C. Time: O(V * E), Space: O(V^3)
D. Time: O(2^V), Space: O(1)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Floyd-Warshall uses three nested loops over vertices (k, i, j) each running V times, taking O(V^3) time and maintaining a V x V distance matrix (O(V^2) space).
