Question:
What is the standard dynamic programming recurrence for Levenshtein distance dp[i][j] (where cost of substitute is 1 if s1[i-1] != s2[j-1] else 0)?

Options:
A. dp[i][j] = min(dp[i-1][j] + 1, dp[i][j-1] + 1, dp[i-1][j-1] + cost)
B. dp[i][j] = dp[i-1][j-1] + dp[i-1][j]
C. dp[i][j] = max(dp[i-1][j], dp[i][j-1])
D. dp[i][j] = dp[i-1][j] * dp[i][j-1]

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
dp[i-1][j] + 1 corresponds to deletion, dp[i][j-1] + 1 corresponds to insertion, and dp[i-1][j-1] + cost corresponds to matching or substitution.
