Question:
What is the capacity of a cut (S, T) in a flow network?

Options:
A. The sum of capacities of all directed edges originating in set S and terminating in set T
B. The sum of all edges in the entire graph
C. The capacity of the smallest edge in S
D. The number of edges crossing from T to S

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Cut capacity is defined strictly as sum_{u in S, v in T} c(u, v). Edges in the reverse direction (from T to S) do not contribute to the cut capacity.
