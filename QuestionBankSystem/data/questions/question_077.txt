Question:
In a d-ary heap where each node has d children, how does increasing d affect the time complexities of insert and extract-min?

Options:
A. Insert (sift-up) becomes faster O(log_d n); extract-min (sift-down) becomes slower O(d * log_d n)
B. Both operations become strictly O(1)
C. Both operations become strictly slower
D. Insert becomes O(d log n); extract-min becomes O(1)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Tree height decreases to log_d(n), making sift-up faster (fewer parent comparisons). However, sift-down must inspect d children per level, taking d * log_d(n) comparisons.
