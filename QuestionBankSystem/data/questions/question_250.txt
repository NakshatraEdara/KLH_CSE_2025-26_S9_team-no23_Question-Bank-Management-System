Question:
What is the time complexity of Kasai's algorithm for constructing the LCP array given a string and its Suffix Array?

Options:
A. O(n) linear time
B. O(n log n)
C. O(n^2)
D. O(n^3)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Kasai's algorithm processes suffixes in original text order. Since the LCP value decreases by at most 1 when advancing from text position i to i + 1, the matching pointer increments at most 2n times, proving O(n) time.
