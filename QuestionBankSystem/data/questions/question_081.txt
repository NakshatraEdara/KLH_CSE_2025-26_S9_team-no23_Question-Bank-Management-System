Question:
What is another common name for a Fenwick Tree?

Options:
A. Binary Indexed Tree (BIT)
B. Red-Black Tree
C. B-Tree
D. Suffix Tree

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A Fenwick Tree is widely known as a Binary Indexed Tree (BIT), introduced by Peter Fenwick in 1994 for efficient cumulative frequency calculations.
