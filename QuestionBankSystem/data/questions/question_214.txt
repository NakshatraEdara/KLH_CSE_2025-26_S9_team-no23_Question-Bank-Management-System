Question:
In generating all permutations of an array of n distinct elements via backtracking, what is the total number of leaves in the recursion tree?

Options:
A. n!
B. 2^n
C. n^2
D. n^n

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The first position has n choices, the second n-1, ..., down to 1. The total number of valid full-length permutations generated at the leaves is n!.
