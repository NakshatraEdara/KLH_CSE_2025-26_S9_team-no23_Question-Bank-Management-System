Question:
Why can the generic Ford-Fulkerson algorithm loop infinitely or fail to converge to the maximum flow when capacities are irrational numbers?

Options:
A. Path choices using DFS can cause flow augmentations to converge to an infinite sum strictly strictly below maximum capacity
B. Irrational numbers cannot be represented in binary
C. The residual graph becomes disconnected
D. DFS causes an immediate divide-by-zero error

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Pathological augmenting path selections on graphs with irrational capacities can generate an infinite sequence of decreasing augmentations that converges to a value far below true max flow.
