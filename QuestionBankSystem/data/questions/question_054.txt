Question:
Which rotation is performed when an imbalance occurs due to an insertion into the right subtree of the right child (RR case)?

Options:
A. A single Left rotation on the unbalanced node
B. A single Right rotation on the unbalanced node
C. A Left-Right double rotation
D. A Right-Left double rotation

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A single Left rotation raises the right child to become the new root of the subtree, restoring height balance when an insertion occurs in the outer right grandchild.
