Question:
What is the FM-index, and what data structures combine to make it an ultra-compact compressed full-text index?

Options:
A. Burrows-Wheeler Transform combined with Wavelet Matrix or checkpointed rank/select bitvectors (O(n) compressed space)
B. A Segment Tree stored on disk
C. A Bloom Filter inside a Hash Table
D. An AVL tree of suffix arrays

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The FM-index combines the BWT with auxiliary rank/select structures (LF-mapping). It enables pattern searches in O(m) time directly within compressed text (often smaller than the original text size).
