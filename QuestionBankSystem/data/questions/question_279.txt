Question:
What are the two fundamental constraints every valid network flow f must satisfy for all edges (u, v) and internal vertices?

Options:
A. Capacity Constraint (0 <= f(u, v) <= c(u, v)) and Flow Conservation (inflow = outflow for all vertices except source and sink)
B. Cycle Constraint and Negative Flow Constraint
C. Degree Constraint and Coloring Constraint
D. All edges must carry equal flow

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Capacity constraint ensures flow does not exceed pipe capacity. Conservation of flow guarantees that no flow is created or destroyed at any intermediate vertex.
