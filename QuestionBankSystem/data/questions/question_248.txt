Question:
Which algorithms construct the Suffix Array in guaranteed linear O(n) worst-case time?

Options:
A. SA-IS (Suffix Array by Induced Sorting) and DC3 (Karkkainen-Sanders / Skew algorithm)
B. Manber-Myers and QuickSort
C. KMP and Rabin-Karp
D. Floyd-Warshall and Prim

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Both DC3 (difference cover 3) and SA-IS (induced sorting) achieve optimal O(n) construction time. SA-IS is widely recognized as the most memory-efficient and practically fastest linear-time algorithm.
