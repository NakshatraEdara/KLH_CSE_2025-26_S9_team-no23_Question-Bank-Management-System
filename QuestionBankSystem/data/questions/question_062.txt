Question:
For a zero-indexed array representation of a binary heap, what are the indices of the left and right children of the element at index i?

Options:
A. Left: 2*i + 1, Right: 2*i + 2
B. Left: 2*i, Right: 2*i + 1
C. Left: i/2, Right: i/2 + 1
D. Left: i + 1, Right: i + 2

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
In 0-indexed complete binary tree mapping, node i has left child at 2*i + 1, right child at 2*i + 2, and parent at floor((i - 1) / 2).
