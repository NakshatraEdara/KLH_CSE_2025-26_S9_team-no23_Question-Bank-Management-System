Question:
What does the Longest Prefix Suffix (LPS) array (also called the pi table) store in the KMP algorithm?

Options:
A. lps[i] stores the length of the longest proper prefix of pattern[0..i] that is also a suffix of pattern[0..i]
B. lps[i] stores the total number of characters in the text
C. lps[i] stores the ASCII value of character i
D. lps[i] stores the hash value of pattern[0..i]

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
By definition, lps[i] holds the length of the longest proper prefix of P[0..i] that matches a suffix of P[0..i]. This determines where to resume matching when a mismatch occurs.
