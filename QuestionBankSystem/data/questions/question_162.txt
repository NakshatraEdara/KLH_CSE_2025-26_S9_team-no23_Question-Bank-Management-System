Question:
Why does Bellman-Ford perform exactly V - 1 relaxation passes over all edges?

Options:
A. A simple shortest path in a graph with V vertices contains at most V - 1 edges; each pass extends the shortest path by at least one edge
B. Because there are V - 1 vertices in total
C. Because the maximum degree of any node is V - 1
D. To ensure memory buffers are cleared

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Without negative cycles, a shortest path visits each vertex at most once and thus has at most V - 1 edges. The k-th relaxation pass guarantees correct shortest paths for all paths using <= k edges.
