Question:
What happens when a leaf node splits in a B+ Tree upon insertion?

Options:
A. The leaf splits into two leaves, the first key of the right leaf is copied (retained) in the leaf and also copied into the parent
B. The median key is removed from the leaves entirely
C. Both leaves are discarded
D. The tree height is tripled

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Because leaves must store all data keys, the promoted key cannot be removed from the leaf level; it is retained in the right leaf and duplicated in the parent index node.
