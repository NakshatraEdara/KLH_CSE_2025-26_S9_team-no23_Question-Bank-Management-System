Question:
In exam question paper generation with bitmask DP, how is topic coverage represented for an exam targeting k required topics?

Options:
A. As an integer bitmask of length k, where the j-th bit is 1 if topic j is covered by the selected questions and 0 otherwise
B. As a floating point array
C. As an adjacency list of strings
D. As an unweighted bipartite graph

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
With k topics (e.g. k <= 20), an integer mask from 0 to 2^k - 1 represents the exact subset of topics covered. Transitions compute mask | question_topics_mask in O(1) bitwise operations.
