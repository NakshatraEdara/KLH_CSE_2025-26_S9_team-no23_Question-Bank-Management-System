Question:
What is the best-case time complexity of Insertion Sort when the input array is already completely sorted?

Options:
A. O(1)
B. O(n)
C. O(n log n)
D. O(n^2)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
When already sorted, the inner loop of insertion sort immediately terminates after 1 comparison per element, yielding O(n) best-case time.
