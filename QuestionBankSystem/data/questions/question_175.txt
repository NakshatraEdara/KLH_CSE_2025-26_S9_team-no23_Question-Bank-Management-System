Question:
How does the Floyd-Warshall algorithm detect the presence of a negative cycle in the graph?

Options:
A. If dist[i][i] < 0 for any vertex i after all iterations complete
B. If any distance equals positive infinity
C. If the determinant of the matrix is negative
D. If all distances are zero

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The distance from any vertex i to itself starts at 0. If dist[i][i] becomes strictly negative, a cycle from i back to i has negative total weight.
