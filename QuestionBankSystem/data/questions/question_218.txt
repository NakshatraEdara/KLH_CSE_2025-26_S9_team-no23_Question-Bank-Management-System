Question:
In the m-Coloring problem (color graph vertices with at most m colors so no adjacent vertices share a color), when does backtracking prune a branch?

Options:
A. When the chosen color for the current vertex matches the color already assigned to any of its adjacent neighbors
B. When the sum of colors exceeds m
C. When a vertex has an odd degree
D. When the chromatic number is 2

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Before assigning color c to vertex v, the algorithm checks all neighbors of v. If any neighbor already has color c, that branch is pruned immediately.
