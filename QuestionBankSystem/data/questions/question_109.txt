Question:
In a 3-level B+ Tree (root, one internal level, leaf level) with order 100 where each leaf holds 50 records, what is the maximum number of records the tree can index?

Options:
A. 100 * 100 * 50 = 500,000 records
B. 5,000 records
C. 50,000,000 records
D. 1,000 records

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Root can point to 100 children. Each of those 100 internal nodes can point to 100 leaves, making 10,000 leaves. With 50 records per leaf, 10,000 * 50 = 500,000 records.
