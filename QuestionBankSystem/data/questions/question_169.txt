Question:
How does Bidirectional Dijkstra accelerate finding the shortest path between a specific start vertex s and target vertex t?

Options:
A. By running two simultaneous searches: forward from s and backward from t, stopping when frontiers meet
B. By running Dijkstra twice from s
C. By halving all edge weights
D. By deleting non-shortest paths

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Searching forward from s and backward from t explores two search balls of radius d/2 instead of one ball of radius d, reducing the total search space from pi*d^2 to 2*pi*(d/2)^2, roughly halving visited vertices.
