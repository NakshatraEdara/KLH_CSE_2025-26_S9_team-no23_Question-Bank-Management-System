Question:
In the Median-of-Medians selection algorithm, dividing elements into groups of 5 guarantees what worst-case time complexity for finding the k-th smallest element?

Options:
A. O(n)
B. O(n log n)
C. O(n^(1.5))
D. O(log n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Groups of 5 ensure the pivot partitions at least 30% of elements on each side. The recurrence T(n) <= T(n/5) + T(7n/10) + O(n) satisfies n/5 + 7n/10 = 9n/10 < n, proving O(n) worst-case time.
