Question:
What is the relationship between the Maximum Bottleneck Spanning Tree (MBST) and the Minimum Spanning Tree (MST)?

Options:
A. Every MST is also a Minimum Bottleneck Spanning Tree (minimizes the maximum edge weight in the tree)
B. An MST never minimizes bottleneck edge weight
C. The MST maximizes the bottleneck edge weight
D. They have disjoint edge sets

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Any Minimum Spanning Tree also minimizes the maximum edge weight across the tree (bottleneck spanning tree), a direct consequence of the Cycle Property.
