Question:
How can a flow network with multiple source vertices and multiple sink vertices be transformed into a standard single-source single-sink network?

Options:
A. By adding a supersource connected to all sources with their supply capacities, and a supersink connected from all sinks with their demand capacities
B. By merging all sources into a single vertex and deleting all incoming edges
C. By running BFS independently for every source-sink pair
D. Multiple sources and sinks cannot be reduced to single-source networks

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A virtual supersource with directed edges to all individual sources, and a virtual supersink with edges from all sinks, standardizes multi-source multi-sink problems into single s-t maximum flow.
