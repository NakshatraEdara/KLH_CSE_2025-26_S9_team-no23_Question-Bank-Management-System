Question:
In Dijkstra's algorithm, what initial distance is assigned to the source vertex and all other vertices?

Options:
A. Source: 0, All other vertices: positive infinity
B. Source: 1, All other vertices: 0
C. All vertices: 0
D. All vertices: negative infinity

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Initialization sets dist[source] = 0 (distance to itself) and dist[v] = infinity for all other vertices v, representing that they are initially unreachable.
