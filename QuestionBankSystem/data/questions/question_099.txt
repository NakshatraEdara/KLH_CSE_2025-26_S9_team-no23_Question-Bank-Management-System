Question:
Why are B-Trees and B+ Trees preferred over AVL or Red-Black trees for disk-based storage and databases?

Options:
A. Their high branching factor results in very shallow trees, minimizing expensive disk I/O reads
B. They can only be stored in CPU registers
C. They do not store any keys in memory
D. They eliminate all disk write operations

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Disk seeks are orders of magnitude slower than RAM access. Large node capacity matching disk page sizes produces tree heights of 3 or 4 for millions of records, drastically reducing disk seeks.
