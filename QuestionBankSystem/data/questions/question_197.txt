Question:
What is the time complexity of the standard Matrix Chain Multiplication dynamic programming algorithm for n matrices?

Options:
A. O(n^3)
B. O(n^2)
C. O(2^n)
D. O(n!)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Computing optimal parenthesization evaluates chain lengths from 2 to n. For each sub-chain (i, j), it tests all split points k between i and j, resulting in O(n^3) total operations.
