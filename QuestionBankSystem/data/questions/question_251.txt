Question:
How can pattern matching for pattern P of length m be performed using a Suffix Array SA of text of length n?

Options:
A. Binary search on SA in O(m log n) time, because suffixes are lexicographically sorted
B. Linear scan through the array in O(n * m)
C. Hashing each suffix into a separate hash table
D. Running BFS on the SA indices

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Because suffixes in SA are sorted alphabetically, binary search can find the range of suffixes having P as a prefix by comparing P with suffixes at midpoints in O(m log n) time.
