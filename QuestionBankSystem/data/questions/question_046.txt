Question:
Which rotation is required to rebalance an AVL tree when an insertion causes a Left-Right (LR) imbalance at a node?

Options:
A. A Left rotation on the left child, followed by a Right rotation on the unbalanced node
B. A single Right rotation on the unbalanced node
C. A single Left rotation on the unbalanced node
D. A Right rotation on the right child, followed by a Left rotation on the unbalanced node

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
An LR imbalance occurs when a key is inserted into the right subtree of the left child. Rebalancing requires a double rotation: Left rotate on child, then Right rotate on parent.
