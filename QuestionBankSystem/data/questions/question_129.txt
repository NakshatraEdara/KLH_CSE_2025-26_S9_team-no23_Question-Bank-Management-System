Question:
What is the necessary and sufficient condition for a connected undirected graph to contain an Euler Circuit?

Options:
A. Every vertex has an even degree
B. Exactly two vertices have odd degree
C. The graph must be bipartite
D. The graph must have no cycles

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
According to Euler's theorem, a connected undirected graph has an Euler circuit if and only if every vertex has an even degree.
