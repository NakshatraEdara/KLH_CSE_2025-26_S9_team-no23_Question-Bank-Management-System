Question:
In a B-Tree of order m, what is the minimum number of children a non-root internal node must possess?

Options:
A. ceil(m / 2)
B. m / 2 - 1
C. 2
D. m - 1

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
To maintain high storage density and balance, every non-root internal node in a B-Tree of order m must have at least ceil(m / 2) children.
