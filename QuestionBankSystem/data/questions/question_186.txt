Question:
How can the actual shortest path sequence between vertices i and j be reconstructed in Floyd-Warshall?

Options:
A. By maintaining a predecessor matrix next[i][j] updated to next[i][k] whenever dist[i][j] is improved via k
B. By re-running BFS from i to j
C. By sorting the distance matrix rows
D. Paths cannot be reconstructed from Floyd-Warshall

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Storing next[i][j] (the first step on the shortest path from i to j) and updating next[i][j] = next[i][k] during relaxation allows path reconstruction in O(path length) time.
