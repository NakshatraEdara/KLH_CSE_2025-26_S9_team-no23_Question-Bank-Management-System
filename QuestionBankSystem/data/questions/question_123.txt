Question:
How does Tarjan's Strongly Connected Components (SCC) algorithm identify SCCs in a directed graph in a single DFS pass?

Options:
A. By maintaining discovery time (disc) and lowest reachable discovery time (low) on a DFS stack; root of an SCC has disc[u] == low[u]
B. By running Dijkstra from every vertex
C. By squaring the adjacency matrix
D. By performing 2^V randomized BFS traversals

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Tarjan's algorithm uses disc and low values computed in DFS. When backtracking finds low[u] == disc[u], node u is the root of an SCC, and popping the DFS stack down to u yields the SCC in O(V + E) time.
