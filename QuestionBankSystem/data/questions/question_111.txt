Question:
What is a 2-3-4 Tree, and what binary search tree is directly isomorphic to it?

Options:
A. A B-Tree of order 4, isomorphic to a Red-Black Tree
B. An AVL tree with balance factor 4
C. A Splay tree
D. A Trie of depth 4

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A 2-3-4 tree (order 4 B-tree) has a 1-to-1 structural correspondence with Red-Black trees, where 3-nodes and 4-nodes are represented by clusters of red-linked nodes.
