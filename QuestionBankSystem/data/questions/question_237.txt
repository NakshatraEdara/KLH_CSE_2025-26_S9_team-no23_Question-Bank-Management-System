Question:
How is the rolling hash updated in O(1) time when sliding a window of length m by one character (dropping old char c_out and adding new char c_in) with base B and modulus M?

Options:
A. H_new = ((H_old - c_out * B^(m-1)) * B + c_in) % M
B. H_new = (H_old + c_in - c_out) % M
C. H_new = (H_old * c_in / c_out) % M
D. H_new = (H_old ^ c_in) % M

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The highest-order term (c_out * B^(m-1)) is subtracted, the remainder is multiplied by base B to shift all positional exponents up by 1, and the new low-order character c_in is added.
