Question:
Which sorting algorithm repeatedly finds the minimum element from the unsorted sublist and swaps it with the first unsorted element?

Options:
A. Selection Sort
B. Merge Sort
C. Radix Sort
D. Insertion Sort

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Selection sort divides the list into sorted and unsorted regions, repeatedly identifying the minimum element from the unsorted region to place at the boundary.
