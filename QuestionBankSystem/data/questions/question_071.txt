Question:
What is the time complexity of the extract-min operation in a standard binary Min-Heap of size n?

Options:
A. O(log n)
B. O(1)
C. O(n)
D. O(n log n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Extract-min replaces the root with the last element in O(1) time and then sifts it down along the tree height, which requires at most O(log n) swaps.
