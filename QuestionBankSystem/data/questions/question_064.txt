Question:
Which advanced heap data structure supports decrease-key in O(1) amortized time and extract-min in O(log n) amortized time?

Options:
A. Fibonacci Heap
B. Binary Heap
C. d-ary Heap
D. Leftist Heap

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Fibonacci heaps use lazy structural updates and cascading cuts, enabling O(1) amortized insertion, union, and decrease-key, which optimizes Dijkstra's algorithm to O(E + V log V).
