Question:
What is edge relaxation in shortest path algorithms?

Options:
A. If dist[u] + weight(u, v) < dist[v], update dist[v] = dist[u] + weight(u, v)
B. Deleting edge (u, v) from the graph
C. Setting weight(u, v) to zero
D. Reversing the direction of edge (u, v)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Relaxing an edge (u, v) tests whether traversing through u improves the current best known shortest distance to v; if so, dist[v] is updated.
