Question:
What does the Integrality Theorem for Maximum Flow guarantee?

Options:
A. If all edge capacities in the network are integers, there exists an optimal maximum flow where every edge carries an integer amount of flow
B. The number of vertices must be an integer
C. All paths must have integer lengths
D. The minimum cut must be a prime number

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Because augmentations in Ford-Fulkerson or Dinic add/subtract integer bottleneck capacities at each step, integer capacity networks always produce strictly integral maximum flows.
