Question:
What is the worst-case time complexity of standard Binary Search on a sorted array of size n?

Options:
A. O(1)
B. O(log n)
C. O(n)
D. O(n log n)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
Binary search divides the search interval in half with each comparison, taking at most floor(log2 n) + 1 steps, which is O(log n).
