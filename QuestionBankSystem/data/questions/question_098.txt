Question:
What is the primary advantage of a Segment Tree over a Fenwick Tree?

Options:
A. Segment Trees can easily support arbitrary associative operations (like range min/max) without requiring invertibility
B. Segment Trees use significantly less memory than Fenwick Trees
C. Segment Trees are easier to implement in 5 lines of code
D. Segment Trees do not require recursion or arrays

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A Fenwick tree requires the operation to have an inverse (like subtraction for addition) to compute range queries [L, R] = prefix(R) - prefix(L-1). Segment trees handle non-invertible operations like min and max natively.
