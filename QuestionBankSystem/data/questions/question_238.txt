Question:
What technique eliminates spurious hash collisions and makes Rabin-Karp virtually immune to worst-case adversarial inputs?

Options:
A. Double Hashing: computing two independent rolling hashes with different large prime moduli and bases
B. Using 16-bit integer hashes
C. Setting modulus M = 2
D. Sorting characters before hashing

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Using two large primes (e.g. 10^9 + 7 and 10^9 + 9) reduces collision probability to ~1 / (10^18), making accidental false matches virtually zero.
