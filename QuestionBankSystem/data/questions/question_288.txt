Question:
What is the time complexity of the highest-label push-relabel algorithm with gap heuristic?

Options:
A. O(V^2 * sqrt(E))
B. O(V * E^2)
C. O(2^V)
D. O(V^4)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Selecting the active node with the maximum height to push excess bounds non-saturating pushes to O(V^2 * sqrt(E)), making it one of the fastest practical maximum flow algorithms.
