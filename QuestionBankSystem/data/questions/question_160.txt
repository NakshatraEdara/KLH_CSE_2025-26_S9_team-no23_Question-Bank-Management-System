Question:
What is the primary capability of the Bellman-Ford algorithm that standard Dijkstra's algorithm lacks?

Options:
A. Handling negative edge weights and detecting negative weight cycles
B. Finding minimum cut in bipartite graphs
C. Generating spanning forests in O(1) time
D. Sorting vertices topologically

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Bellman-Ford correctly computes single-source shortest paths in the presence of negative edge weights and can detect the presence of negative weight cycles reachable from the source.
