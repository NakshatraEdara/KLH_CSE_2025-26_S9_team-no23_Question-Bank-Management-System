Question:
What is the maximum number of edges in a simple undirected graph with V vertices?

Options:
A. V * (V - 1) / 2
B. V^2
C. 2 * V
D. V - 1

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Each of the V vertices can connect to V - 1 other vertices. In an undirected graph without self-loops or multi-edges, total edges is V * (V - 1) / 2.
