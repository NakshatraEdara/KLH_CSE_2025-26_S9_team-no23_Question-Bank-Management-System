Question:
What is the time complexity of the Hopcroft-Karp algorithm for finding maximum cardinality bipartite matching?

Options:
A. O(E * sqrt(V))
B. O(V * E)
C. O(V^3)
D. O(E log V)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Hopcroft-Karp repeatedly finds a maximal set of shortest vertex-disjoint augmenting paths in each phase using BFS and DFS, terminating in at most O(sqrt(V)) phases for a total time of O(E * sqrt(V)).
