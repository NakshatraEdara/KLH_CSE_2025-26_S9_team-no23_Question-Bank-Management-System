Question:
What is the minimum number of nodes in an AVL tree of height 4 (where a single-node tree has height 0)?

Options:
A. 12
B. 15
C. 7
D. 31

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Minimum nodes follows N(h) = N(h-1) + N(h-2) + 1. Base cases: N(0)=1, N(1)=2, N(2)=4, N(3)=7, N(4) = 7 + 4 + 1 = 12 nodes.
