Question:
What are PAM and BLOSUM matrices in sequence alignment?

Options:
A. Substitution scoring matrices representing log-odds evolutionary replacement probabilities between amino acids
B. Compressed hash tables for DNA strings
C. Random number generators for simulated annealing
D. Binary trees used for sorting sequences

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
PAM (Point Accepted Mutation) and BLOSUM (Blocks Substitution Matrix) quantify the biological likelihood of replacing one amino acid with another based on observed evolutionary conservation.
