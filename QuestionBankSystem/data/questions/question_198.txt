Question:
How does the Knuth-Yao optimization reduce the time complexity of certain interval DP problems (like optimal BST) from O(n^3) to O(n^2)?

Options:
A. By utilizing the monotonicity of optimal split points: opt[i][j-1] <= opt[i][j] <= opt[i+1][j]
B. By converting the problem to maximum flow
C. By using bitsets to parallelize evaluations
D. By running FFT on matrix entries

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
When cost functions satisfy the quadrangle inequality and monotonicity, the optimal split point opt[i][j] is bounded between opt[i][j-1] and opt[i+1][j], making the sum of split searches telescope to O(n^2).
