Question:
What does the Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem state?

Options:
A. The maximum amount of flow passing from source to sink is strictly equal to the minimum total capacity of an s-t cut in the network
B. Max flow is equal to the number of vertices
C. Max flow equals the sum of all edge capacities in the network
D. The minimum cut always contains exactly two edges

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The fundamental theorem of network flows proves that the maximum flow achievable from source s to sink t equals the minimum capacity among all valid cuts separating s and t.
