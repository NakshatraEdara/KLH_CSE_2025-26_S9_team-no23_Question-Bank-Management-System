Question:
What is the time complexity of Kruskal's algorithm on a graph with V vertices and E edges?

Options:
A. O(E log E) or O(E log V)
B. O(V^2)
C. O(V * E)
D. O(V + E)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Sorting E edges takes O(E log E). The DSU operations take O(E * alpha(V)), which is dominated by sorting, yielding overall time O(E log E) = O(E log V).
