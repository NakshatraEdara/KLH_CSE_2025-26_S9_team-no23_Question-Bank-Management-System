Question:
What is the worst-case time complexity of merging k sorted singly linked lists having a total of N nodes using a min-heap priority queue?

Options:
A. O(N log k)
B. O(N * k)
C. O(k log N)
D. O(N^2)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Maintaining a min-heap of size k containing the head of each list takes O(log k) per step. For N total nodes extracted, the overall time complexity is O(N log k).
