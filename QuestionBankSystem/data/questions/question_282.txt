Question:
What is the time complexity of the Edmonds-Karp algorithm for maximum flow on a network with V vertices and E edges?

Options:
A. O(V * E^2)
B. O(V^2 * E)
C. O(V + E)
D. O(E * log V)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Edmonds-Karp implements the Ford-Fulkerson method by finding shortest augmenting paths (in edge count) using BFS. There are at most O(V * E) augmentations, each taking O(E) BFS time, totaling O(V * E^2).
