Question:
In the worst case, how many tree rotations are required to restore AVL balance after deleting a single node?

Options:
A. O(log n) rotations
B. At most 1 single or double rotation
C. O(n) rotations
D. Zero rotations

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
While insertion rebalancing terminates after at most 1 single or double rotation, deletion can propagate height changes up the entire tree, requiring O(log n) rotations.
