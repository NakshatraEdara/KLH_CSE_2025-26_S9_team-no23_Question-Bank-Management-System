Question:
When dynamic arrays (such as Java's ArrayList) double their capacity upon becoming full, what is the amortized time complexity of an append operation?

Options:
A. O(n)
B. O(1)
C. O(log n)
D. O(n log n)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
Although doubling takes O(n) time, resizing occurs infrequently enough that the total cost over n insertions averages out to O(1) amortized time per insertion.
