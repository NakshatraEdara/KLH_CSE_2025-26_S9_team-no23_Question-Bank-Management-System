Question:
How many standard FIFO queues are required to implement a LIFO Stack where push or pop is O(1)?

Options:
A. Exactly 1 queue
B. 2 queues
C. 3 queues
D. It is impossible to simulate a stack using queues

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
Two queues can simulate a stack: one acts as the main storage, and the second is used during push (or pop) to reverse element order.
