Question:
What is the time complexity of the standard Bellman-Ford algorithm on a graph with V vertices and E edges?

Options:
A. O(V * E)
B. O(V + E)
C. O(E log V)
D. O(V^3)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Bellman-Ford relaxes all E edges V - 1 times, resulting in a worst-case time complexity of O(V * E).
