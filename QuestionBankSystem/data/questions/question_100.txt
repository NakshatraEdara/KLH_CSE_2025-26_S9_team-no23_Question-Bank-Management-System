Question:
In a B-Tree of order m, what is the maximum number of children any internal node can have?

Options:
A. m
B. m - 1
C. 2 * m
D. ceil(m / 2)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
By definition, a B-tree of order m allows an internal node to have at most m children and at most m - 1 keys.
