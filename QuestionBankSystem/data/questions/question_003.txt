Question:
In Kadane's algorithm for finding the maximum subarray sum in an array containing both positive and negative integers, what is the optimal time and auxiliary space complexity?

Options:
A. Time O(n) and Auxiliary Space O(1)
B. Time O(n log n) and Auxiliary Space O(n)
C. Time O(n^2) and Auxiliary Space O(1)
D. Time O(n) and Auxiliary Space O(n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Kadane's algorithm computes the maximum subarray sum in a single linear pass using two running scalar variables, achieving O(n) time and O(1) auxiliary space.
