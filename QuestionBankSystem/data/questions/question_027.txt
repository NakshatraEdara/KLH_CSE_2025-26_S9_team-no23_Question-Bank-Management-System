Question:
What is the lower bound on the number of comparisons required to sort n items in the comparison-based sorting model in the worst case?

Options:
A. Omega(log n)
B. Omega(n)
C. Omega(n log n)
D. Omega(n^2)

Correct Answer: C

Explanation:
A decision tree sorting n elements must have at least n! leaves. The height of the binary decision tree is at least ceil(log2(n!)) = Omega(n log n) by Stirling's approximation.
