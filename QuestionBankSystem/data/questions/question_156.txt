Question:
What is the time complexity of Dijkstra's algorithm when implemented with a Fibonacci Heap?

Options:
A. O(E + V log V)
B. O(E log V)
C. O(V^2)
D. O(E * V)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
With O(1) amortized decrease-key operations for E edges and O(log V) amortized extract-min operations for V vertices, the total runtime is O(E + V log V).
