Question:
What is the time complexity of Dijkstra's algorithm using an adjacency list and a standard binary min-heap?

Options:
A. O((V + E) log V)
B. O(V^2)
C. O(V * E)
D. O(V + E)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Extract-min is called V times (O(V log V)) and edge relaxations insert or decrease keys at most E times (O(E log V)), yielding O((V + E) log V).
