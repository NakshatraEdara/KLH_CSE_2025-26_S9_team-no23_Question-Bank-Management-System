Question:
What is the condition for a heuristic h(n) to be consistent (monotonic)?

Options:
A. For every node n and neighbor n' with edge cost c(n, n'), h(n) <= c(n, n') + h(n'), and h(goal) = 0
B. h(n) >= 2 * c(n, n')
C. h(n) must be constant everywhere
D. h(n) must equal the depth of node n

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Consistency is the triangle inequality for heuristics: the estimated cost to goal from n cannot exceed the step cost to n' plus estimated cost from n' to goal. Consistency implies admissibility.
