Question:
What is a Residual Graph in maximum flow algorithms?

Options:
A. A graph containing forward edges with residual capacity c(u, v) - f(u, v) and backward edges with capacity f(u, v) representing flow cancellation
B. The graph of unused vertices
C. A subgraph with negative edge weights
D. A tree with no directed edges

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The residual graph indicates remaining available capacity on forward edges and reverse capacity on backward edges, allowing subsequent augmenting paths to 'undo' earlier suboptimal flow choices.
