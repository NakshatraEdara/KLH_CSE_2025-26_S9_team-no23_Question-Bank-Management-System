Question:
In an iterative (bottom-up) Segment Tree of size n with leaves stored at indices [n, 2n - 1], what are the left and right children of node p?

Options:
A. Left: 2 * p, Right: 2 * p + 1
B. Left: p - 1, Right: p + 1
C. Left: p / 2, Right: p / 2 + 1
D. Left: p + n, Right: p + n + 1

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
In an iterative segment tree, node p has left child at 2*p, right child at 2*p + 1, and parent at p >> 1, eliminating recursion and pointer overhead.
