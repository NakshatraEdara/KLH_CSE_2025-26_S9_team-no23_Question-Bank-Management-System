Question:
What is the space complexity of standard in-place HeapSort?

Options:
A. O(1)
B. O(log n)
C. O(n)
D. O(n log n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
HeapSort constructs a heap within the input array itself and performs sift-down operations iteratively using only O(1) auxiliary space.
