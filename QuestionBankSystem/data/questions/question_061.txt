Question:
What is the fundamental invariant of a Max-Heap?

Options:
A. The key of every parent node is greater than or equal to the keys of its children
B. Left child key is always less than right child key
C. Every node must have exactly two non-null children
D. Leaves must be sorted in ascending order

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
In a max-heap, the max-heap property dictates that key(parent) >= key(child) for every node other than the root.
