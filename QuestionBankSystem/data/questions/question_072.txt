Question:
In Cuckoo Hashing with two independent hash functions h1 and h2, what happens when an insertion triggers a displacement cycle?

Options:
A. An infinite loop is detected and the entire table is rehashed with new hash functions and larger capacity
B. The key is permanently discarded
C. The table switches to linear search
D. A third table is dynamically allocated

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Cuckoo hashing guarantees O(1) worst-case lookup by checking at most two locations. If an insertion enters an infinite displacement loop, rehash with fresh random hash functions is triggered.
