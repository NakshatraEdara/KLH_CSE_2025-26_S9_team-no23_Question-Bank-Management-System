Question:
What is Kahn's algorithm for Topological Sorting of a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)?

Options:
A. A BFS-based algorithm that repeatedly enqueues vertices with an in-degree of 0 and removes their outgoing edges
B. An algorithm that sorts vertices by their labels
C. A randomized DFS that visits sinks first
D. A priority queue algorithm based on vertex degrees

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Kahn's algorithm calculates in-degrees for all vertices, initializes a queue with all in-degree 0 vertices, and repeatedly decrements neighbor in-degrees as vertices are dequeued.
