Question:
In the Activity Selection Problem (Interval Scheduling), which greedy strategy yields the maximum number of mutually compatible activities?

Options:
A. Always pick the compatible activity with the earliest finish time
B. Pick the activity with the shortest duration
C. Pick the activity with the earliest start time
D. Pick the activity with the fewest conflicts

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Sorting activities by finish time and greedily choosing the first finishing non-conflicting activity leaves the maximum remaining time available for subsequent activities.
