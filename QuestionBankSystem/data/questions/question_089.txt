Question:
What technique allows a Segment Tree to perform range updates (e.g. add v to all elements in [L, R]) in O(log n) time instead of O(n)?

Options:
A. Lazy Propagation
B. Linear Probing
C. Cascading Cuts
D. Dynamic Sifting

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Lazy propagation postpones updating descendant nodes until they are visited by subsequent queries or updates, storing pending modifications in a lazy array and achieving O(log n) range updates.
