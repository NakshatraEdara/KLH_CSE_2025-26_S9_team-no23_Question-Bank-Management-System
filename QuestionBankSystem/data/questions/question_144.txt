Question:
What does the Cut Property of Minimum Spanning Trees state?

Options:
A. For any cut in the graph, the minimum-weight edge crossing the cut belongs to every MST (if weights are unique)
B. The heaviest edge in any cut must always be in the MST
C. A cut can have at most one edge
D. Every vertex in a cut has degree 2

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The Cut Property states that for any partition of vertices into sets S and V\S, the edge with the strictly minimum weight crossing between S and V\S is part of the MST.
