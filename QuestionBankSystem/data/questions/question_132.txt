Question:
What is 0-1 BFS, and what data structure allows it to find shortest paths in O(V + E) time on graphs with edge weights restricted to {0, 1}?

Options:
A. A double-ended queue (deque); weight-0 edges push to the front, weight-1 edges push to the back
B. A priority queue with Fibonacci heap
C. Two separate stacks
D. A disjoint-set union find

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
In 0-1 BFS, pushing 0-cost transitions to the front and 1-cost transitions to the back keeps the deque sorted by distance, achieving O(V + E) shortest path time without priority queue overhead.
