Question:
During deletion in a B-Tree, what rebalancing operation occurs when a node has fewer than ceil(m / 2) - 1 keys and neither sibling can spare a key?

Options:
A. Node merging: the node, an adjacent sibling, and the dividing key from the parent are merged into a single node
B. Key rotation with grandparent
C. Rehashing the entire tree
D. Converting the underflowing node into a leaf

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
If key borrowing (rotation) from siblings is impossible because siblings are at minimum capacity, the node is merged with a sibling and the separator key from the parent, which may propagate merges upwards.
