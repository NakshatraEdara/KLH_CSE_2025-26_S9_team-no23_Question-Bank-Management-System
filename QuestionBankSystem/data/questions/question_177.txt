Question:
In the A* search algorithm, how is the evaluation function f(n) defined for each candidate node n?

Options:
A. f(n) = g(n) + h(n), where g(n) is the exact cost from start to n, and h(n) is heuristic estimated cost from n to goal
B. f(n) = g(n) - h(n)
C. f(n) = g(n) * h(n)
D. f(n) = max(g(n), h(n))

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A* ranks nodes by f(n) = g(n) + h(n), balancing the known exact traversal cost from the origin g(n) with the estimated prospective cost to the objective h(n).
