Question:
What two optimization heuristics ensure that Disjoint Set Union operations run in nearly linear O(alpha(n)) amortized time?

Options:
A. Path Compression and Union by Rank (or Size)
B. Binary Search and Linear Interpolation
C. Lazy Propagation and Cascading Cuts
D. Bitwise XOR and Radix Sorting

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Path compression flattens tree paths during find operations, and union by rank attaches smaller trees under larger roots, bounding m operations to O(m * alpha(n)) where alpha is the inverse Ackermann function.
