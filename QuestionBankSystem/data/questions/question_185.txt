Question:
For an 8-directional grid where diagonal movements cost 1 (or sqrt(2)), which heuristic is preferred?

Options:
A. Diagonal (Octile or Chebyshev) distance
B. Manhattan distance
C. Topological distance
D. Levenshtein distance

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Octile distance accounts for diagonal moves (costing sqrt(2) or 1) combined with remaining straight moves, providing a tight admissible heuristic for 8-connected grid navigation.
