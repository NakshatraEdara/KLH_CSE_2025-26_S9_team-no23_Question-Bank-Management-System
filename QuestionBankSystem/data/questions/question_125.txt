Question:
In an undirected graph, how does Tarjan's bridge-finding algorithm determine that an edge (u, v) is a bridge (cut-edge)?

Options:
A. When low[v] > disc[u]
B. When low[v] <= disc[u]
C. When low[v] == 0
D. When disc[v] == disc[u]

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
If the lowest discovery time reachable from v's subtree (low[v]) is strictly greater than disc[u], there is no back-edge from v's subtree to u or its ancestors, meaning (u, v) is a bridge.
