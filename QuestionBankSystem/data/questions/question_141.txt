Question:
What is the time complexity of Prim's algorithm when implemented using a binary min-heap priority queue and adjacency lists?

Options:
A. O(E log V)
B. O(V^2)
C. O(E + V)
D. O(V * E)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Each vertex is extracted from the heap once (V * log V), and each edge can trigger a decrease-key/insertion in the heap (E * log V), yielding O((V + E) log V) = O(E log V).
