Question:
Which principle governs the order of insertion and removal in a standard queue?

Options:
A. First In First Out (FIFO)
B. Last In First Out (LIFO)
C. Random Access Only
D. Last In Last Out (LILO)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A queue operates under the First In First Out (FIFO) discipline: items enqueue at the rear and dequeue from the front.
