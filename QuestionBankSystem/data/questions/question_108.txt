Question:
What is true about the depth of all leaf nodes in any valid B-Tree?

Options:
A. All leaf nodes must be at exactly the same depth (perfect height balance)
B. Left leaves must be deeper than right leaves
C. Leaf depths can differ by up to 2 levels
D. Leaves always have depth 1

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A defining structural invariant of B-Trees is that all leaf nodes reside at the exact same depth, ensuring uniform search path lengths.
