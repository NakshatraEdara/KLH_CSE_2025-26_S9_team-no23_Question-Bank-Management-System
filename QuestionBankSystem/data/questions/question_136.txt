Question:
If all edge weights in a connected undirected graph are strictly unique, how many distinct Minimum Spanning Trees can the graph have?

Options:
A. Exactly 1 unique MST
B. V - 1 distinct MSTs
C. 2^(V - 1) distinct MSTs
D. Infinitely many

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A fundamental theorem of graph theory states that if all edge weights in a connected graph are distinct, the Minimum Spanning Tree is strictly unique.
