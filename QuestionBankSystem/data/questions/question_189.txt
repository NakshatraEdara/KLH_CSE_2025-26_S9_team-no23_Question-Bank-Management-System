Question:
What are the two essential characteristics a problem must exhibit for Dynamic Programming to be applicable?

Options:
A. Optimal Substructure and Overlapping Subproblems
B. Greedy Choice Property and Infinite States
C. Total Order and Linear Time Transitions
D. Randomized Steps and Independence

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Dynamic Programming applies when an optimal solution contains within it optimal solutions to subproblems (optimal substructure) and the same subproblems are solved repeatedly (overlapping subproblems).
