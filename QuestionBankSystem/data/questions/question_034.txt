Question:
What is the expected time complexity of the QuickSelect algorithm (Hoare's selection algorithm) to find the k-th smallest element?

Options:
A. O(n)
B. O(n log n)
C. O(n^2)
D. O(k log n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
QuickSelect only recurses into one partition containing the k-th element. The recurrence T(n) = T(n/2) + O(n) resolves to a geometric series yielding O(n) expected time.
