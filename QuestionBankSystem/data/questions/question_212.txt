Question:
What is the state-space search strategy used in a standard Sudoku Solver?

Options:
A. Recursive depth-first backtracking: fill an empty cell with a valid candidate (1-9), recurse, and undo if no solution exists
B. Greedy randomized placement
C. Breadth-first search storing all 9^81 boards in a queue
D. Sorting numbers row by row

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A Sudoku solver finds an empty cell, tries numbers 1 through 9 that satisfy row, column, and 3x3 box constraints, recurses on the remaining cells, and backtracks upon encountering an impasse.
