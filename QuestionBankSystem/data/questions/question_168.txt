Question:
In a DAG (Directed Acyclic Graph) with arbitrary (including negative) weights, how fast can single-source shortest paths be found without Bellman-Ford?

Options:
A. O(V + E) time by relaxing edges in topological order
B. O(V * E)
C. O(V^2)
D. O(2^V)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Because a DAG has no cycles, topologically sorting vertices in O(V + E) and relaxing outgoing edges in that order computes exact shortest (and longest) paths in linear O(V + E) time.
