Question:
What is a Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) of a connected, edge-weighted undirected graph?

Options:
A. A subset of edges that connects all vertices without cycles and minimizes total edge weight
B. A path connecting the two farthest vertices in the graph
C. A cycle containing all vertices with minimum weight
D. A tree with the minimum possible diameter

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
An MST is an acyclic spanning subgraph that connects every vertex in G with exactly V - 1 edges such that the sum of edge weights is minimized.
