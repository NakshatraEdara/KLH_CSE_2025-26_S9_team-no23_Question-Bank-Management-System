Question:
What is the primary objective of an automated exam generation system constrained by total marks M and difficulty breakdown (Easy, Medium, Hard)?

Options:
A. Select a subset of questions whose marks sum exactly to M while satisfying predefined percentage quotas for each difficulty tier
B. Select questions in strictly alphabetical order
C. Ensure every question has the same text length
D. Maximize the number of options per question

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Exam builders optimize multi-criteria constraints: total target marks, difficulty distribution (e.g. 30% Easy, 40% Medium, 30% Hard), and non-overlap across sets.
