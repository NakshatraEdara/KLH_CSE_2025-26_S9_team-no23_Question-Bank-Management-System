Question:
What is the Aho-Corasick string matching algorithm, and what is its time complexity to find all occurrences of a dictionary of patterns of total length M in a text of length N?

Options:
A. O(N + M + Z) where Z is total occurrences, using a Trie augmented with failure transitions (suffix links)
B. O(N * M)
C. O(N^2 + M^2)
D. O(M log N)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Aho-Corasick constructs a finite-state automaton (Trie with BFS failure links resembling KMP). It processes the text in a single pass of O(N) time regardless of the number of dictionary patterns.
