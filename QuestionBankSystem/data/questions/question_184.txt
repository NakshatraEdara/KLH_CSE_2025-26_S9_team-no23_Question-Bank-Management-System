Question:
For a 4-directional grid where movement is restricted to Up, Down, Left, and Right, which distance metric provides a standard admissible heuristic?

Options:
A. Manhattan distance: |x1 - x2| + |y1 - y2|
B. Euclidean distance squared: (x1 - x2)^2 + (y1 - y2)^2
C. Chebyshev distance: max(|x1 - x2|, |y1 - y2|)
D. Hamming distance

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
In grid navigation with only orthogonal moves, the shortest path without obstacles is the Manhattan distance, making it exact and admissible.
