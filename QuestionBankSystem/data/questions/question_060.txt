Question:
What is the time complexity of deleting a node from an AVL tree containing n nodes?

Options:
A. O(log n)
B. O(1)
C. O(n)
D. O(n log n)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Node deletion follows BST deletion in O(log n) time, followed by walking up the ancestral path performing at most O(log n) rotations of O(1) each, yielding O(log n) total time.
