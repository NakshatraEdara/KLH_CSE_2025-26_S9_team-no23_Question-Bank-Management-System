Question:
What mathematical property must an operation satisfy to be maintained across segments in a Segment Tree?

Options:
A. Associativity
B. Commutativity and Nilpotence
C. Linearity only
D. Differentiability

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Associativity (op(op(a, b), c) = op(a, op(b, c))) is strictly required so that results computed over sub-intervals can be combined correctly in arbitrary tree hierarchical groupings.
