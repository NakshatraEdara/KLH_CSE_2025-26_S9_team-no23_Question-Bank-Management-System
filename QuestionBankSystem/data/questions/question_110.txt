Question:
What is a 2-3 Tree in relation to general B-Trees?

Options:
A. A B-Tree of order 3, where every internal node has either 2 children (1 key) or 3 children (2 keys)
B. A binary tree with depth 2 or 3
C. A heap with two roots
D. A tree with 2 leaves and 3 roots

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
A 2-3 tree is a B-tree of order 3. Each internal node is either a 2-node (1 key, 2 children) or a 3-node (2 keys, 3 children).
