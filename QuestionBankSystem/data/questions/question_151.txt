Question:
What is the total number of edges in a Minimum Spanning Tree for a connected graph with 50 vertices?

Options:
A. 49
B. 50
C. 100
D. 1225

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Any spanning tree of a graph with V vertices contains exactly V - 1 edges. For V = 50, the MST has 50 - 1 = 49 edges.
