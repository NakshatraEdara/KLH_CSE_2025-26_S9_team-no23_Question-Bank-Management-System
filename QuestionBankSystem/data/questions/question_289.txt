Question:
How can the minimum s-t cut edges be identified after running a maximum flow algorithm?

Options:
A. Run BFS/DFS from source s in the final residual graph to find all reachable vertices S; the min-cut consists of all original edges from S to V\S with full capacity flow
B. Take all edges with zero flow
C. Take the edges connected directly to the sink
D. Sort edges by capacity

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Vertices reachable from s in the residual graph form the source set S. Original edges directed from S to unreachable vertices V\S are fully saturated, constituting the minimal bottleneck cut.
