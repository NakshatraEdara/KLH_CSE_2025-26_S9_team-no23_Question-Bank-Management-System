Question:
What is the Levenshtein distance between 'kitten' and 'sitting'?

Options:
A. 3 (substitute 'k'->'s', substitute 'e'->'i', insert 'g')
B. 1
C. 2
D. 5

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
1: 'k' -> 's' ('sitten'). 2: 'e' -> 'i' ('sittin'). 3: insert 'g' at end ('sitting'). Total edit operations = 3.
