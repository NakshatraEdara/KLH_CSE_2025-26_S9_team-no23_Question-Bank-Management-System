Question:
What is the time complexity of Radix Sort when sorting n integers with d digits in base b?

Options:
A. O(d * (n + b))
B. O(n log n)
C. O(n^2 * d)
D. O(b * log d)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Radix sort performs d passes of a stable sub-sort (such as Counting Sort). Each pass processes n elements with range b in O(n + b) time, total O(d * (n + b)).
