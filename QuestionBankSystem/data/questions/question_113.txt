Question:
In preemptive (top-down) insertion into a B-Tree, how are node splits handled during the descent?

Options:
A. Any full node encountered on the downward traversal is split immediately before descending into it
B. Nodes are only split after reaching the leaf and backtracking
C. The root is deleted and recreated
D. Nodes are split only when memory reaches 90%

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Top-down B-Tree insertion splits any full node encountered during the root-to-leaf descent. This guarantees the parent always has room to receive a promoted key, avoiding recursive cascading splits.
