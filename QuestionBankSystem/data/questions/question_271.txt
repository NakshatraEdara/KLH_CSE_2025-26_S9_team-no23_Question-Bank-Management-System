Question:
In sequence alignment, what is an Affine Gap Penalty, and why is it biologically realistic?

Options:
A. Penalty = gap_open + (k - 1) * gap_extend; opening a gap is penalized heavily, but extending an existing gap costs less
B. Penalty is proportional to k^2
C. Gaps have zero penalty
D. Only gaps of size 1 are allowed

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Biological insertions and deletions often occur in multi-nucleotide blocks. Penalizing gap opening more than gap extension (Gotoh's algorithm) mirrors natural evolutionary mutation patterns.
