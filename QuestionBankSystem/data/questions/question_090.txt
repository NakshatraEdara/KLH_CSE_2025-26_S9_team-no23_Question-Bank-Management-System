Question:
What is the time complexity of a Range Minimum Query (RMQ) in a standard Segment Tree of size n?

Options:
A. O(log n)
B. O(1)
C. O(n)
D. O(sqrt(n))

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
The query visits at most four nodes per level across the tree height of ceil(log2 n), bounding total RMQ time to O(log n).
