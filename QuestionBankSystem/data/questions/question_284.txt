Question:
What is the time complexity of Dinic's (Dinitz's) algorithm for maximum flow on general networks?

Options:
A. O(V^2 * E)
B. O(V * E^2)
C. O(V^3)
D. O(E * log V)

Correct Answer: A

Explanation:
Dinic's algorithm builds a level graph using BFS and pushes blocking flows along multiple augmenting paths using DFS. There are at most V - 1 phases, and each phase takes O(V * E) time, totaling O(V^2 * E).
