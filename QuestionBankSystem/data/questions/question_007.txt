Question:
Which principle governs the order of insertion and removal in a standard stack data structure?

Options:
A. First In First Out (FIFO)
B. Last In First Out (LIFO)
C. Highest Priority First Out (HPFO)
D. Shortest Remaining Time First (SRTF)

Correct Answer: B

Explanation:
A stack operates strictly on the Last In First Out (LIFO) principle, where the most recently added element is the first one removed.
